# Healthcare-Analytics-Dashboard
# Project Objective
To analyze hospital patient and revenue data and provide actionable insights into healthcare operations, patient demographics, admission patterns, treatment performance, and financial performance through interactive visualizations and KPIs.
# Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/saleem2411/Healthcare-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/main/admissions.csv">Dataset</a>
- <a href="https://github.com/saleem2411/Healthcare-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/main/patients.csv">Dataset</a>
- <a href="https://github.com/saleem2411/Healthcare-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/main/treatments.csv">Dataset</a>

# KPIS
- What is the total revenue generated by the hospital?
- How many patients were treated during the analysis period?
- Which doctor generated the highest revenue?
- Which treatment contributed the most revenue?
- What is the distribution of revenue across admission types?
- Which city contributes the highest number of patients?
- What is the patient distribution by gender?
- Which treatment type is most frequently performed?
- How do patient counts vary across age groups?
- Which admission type handles the highest number of patients?

# Process
- Gathered healthcare dataset from Kaggle.com.
- Performed data cleaning by removing duplicates, handling missing values, and correcting inconsistencies using Power Query.
- Validated patient records to ensure data accuracy and consistency.
- Created relationships between hospital, patient, treatment, and revenue-related data.
- Created DAX measures for Total Revenue, Total Patients, and Revenue per Patient.
- Defined key performance indicators (KPIs): Total Revenue, Total Patients, Revenue by Treatment, and Revenue by Doctor.
- Implemented interactive slicers for: City, Patient Name, and Ward.
- Built visualizations to analyze revenue distribution across doctors, treatments, admission types, genders, and cities.
- Designed an interactive dashboard to support healthcare operational and financial analysis.

# Project Insights
- A total of 100 patients were analyzed.
- The hospital generated approximately ₹2.3 Million in total revenue.
- Surgery is the highest revenue-generating treatment, contributing over ₹650K.
- Dr. Sharma generated the highest revenue among all doctors.
- Patient distribution is concentrated within the age range of 40–60 years.
- Emergency admissions contribute the largest share of total revenue.
- Guntur contributes the highest revenue among all cities.
- Female patients slightly outnumber male patients.
- Emergency admissions account for the highest patient volume.

# Final Conclusion
The analysis reveals that the hospital's financial performance is primarily driven by surgical treatments and emergency admissions. Dr. Sharma contributes the highest revenue, while Guntur accounts for the largest share of both revenue and patient volume. Female patients represent a slightly larger proportion of the patient population, and most patients fall within the middle-age demographic. The dashboard enables healthcare administrators to monitor operational efficiency, identify high-performing services, optimize resource allocation, and make informed decisions to improve patient care and revenue growth.
